# Acknowledgments

The creators of the Latin UMRs wish to express their gratitude to the authors of the Latin Dependency Treebank and the Index Thomisticus Project.

The Latin UMRs contain both manually annotated and automatically converted data.
More detailed release notes on the [project webpage](https://ufal.mff.cuni.cz/uniform-meaning-representation-latin).

# Changes from UMR 2.0 to UMR 2.2

Added partial automatic conversion of the remainder of Sallust, and two texts by Caesar and Cicero.
